Propeller is a **leveraged staking** strategy. You deposit **{props.symbol}** and the vault runs a recursive loop — supplying your collateral, minting a synthetic against it, and re-staking — to amplify the underlying staking yield without you having to manage any positions manually.

There is **no user-facing borrow** and **no liquidation risk** for depositors. The vault keeps its loan-to-value inside a target band and **self-deleverages** automatically when the market moves, so your shares (**{props.shareSymbol}**) simply accrue value as the loop harvests and compounds rewards.

### Key features

- Rewards are automatically compounded into the vault
- No separate claiming required
- No impermanent loss
- No liquidation of your deposited position
- Withdraw all or part of your position at any time

### Risk management

Exits go through an **async withdrawal queue**. When you request a redemption, the amount of {props.symbol} you will receive is **fixed at the share price at the moment of the request** — later deposits, withdrawals or price moves do not change it.

The vault then unwinds the corresponding slice of the loop over subsequent blocks. Your {props.symbol} becomes claimable as it settles, **possibly in parts**, so you may claim more than once for a single request.
